About Us

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Estimated Delivery Timeline

  • Accra (within the city): 1 – 2 business days after order confirmation.
  • Outside Accra (other regions of Ghana): 2 – 5 business days.
  • Orders placed before 3:00 PM GMT are processed the same business day; orders placed after that time are processed the next business day.

Delivery Fees

  • Flat delivery fee of GH₵20 within Accra.
  • FREE delivery on all orders above GH₵500.
  • For locations outside Accra, exact delivery fees are confirmed with you via WhatsApp or phone before your order is dispatched.

Dispatch & Order Tracking

All orders are dispatched directly from our own stock in Accra. Once your order is on its way, you will receive a WhatsApp or phone update with the delivery status. You can also track your order at any time by contacting us on +233 599 121887 with your order reference.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched for delivery. To cancel, contact us on WhatsApp (+233 599 121887) or email dokumkwamevida@gmail.com with your order reference. A full refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Returns (7-Day Return Window)

  • We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for items that are defective, damaged, or not as described.
  • Size exchanges are available within 7 days, provided the item is unworn, unwashed, and in its original packaging with tags attached.
  • All returns must be accompanied by proof of purchase (order reference or receipt).

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed within 3 – 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method (card or Mobile Money) via Paystack, or as store credit if you prefer.
  • Delivery fees are non-refundable, except where the return is due to our error (wrong or damaged item supplied).
